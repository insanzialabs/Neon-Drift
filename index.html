How do I play Neon Drift?

Use arrow keys or A/D to change lanes. On touchscreens, use the left/right buttons or swipe the road. Hold Space or Boost to spend earned energy. Release and refill when the reserve runs out.

How do close calls and armor work?

Pass beside traffic to earn near-miss points and energy. A clean pass is counted only after the vehicle clears your car. The multiplier caps at ×5. One armor charge saves you from a collision. Collect three ion cells to repair spent armor.

What is Cruise pace?

Cruise gives you more time to react and tracks a separate best score. Both paces have three run goals. P pauses, R retries after a crash. Showcase is labeled autopilot and cannot save records.

What is slipstream?

Follow traffic in the same lane, 20–85 metres behind it, for one second to bank a surge. Change lanes to release two seconds of free boost and 400 points. A hit clears the charge. There is no collision protection during a surge.

Midnight Sprint & skill collection

Finish 60 seconds to earn Bronze at 6,000, Silver at 10,000 or Gold at 14,000 points. Crashing does not award a medal. Records and badges are separate for Arcade and Cruise. Earn Wake rider with 3 surges in one run, Close-call artist with 10 near misses, and Midnight gold with a Gold Sprint. Showcase never saves rewards.
